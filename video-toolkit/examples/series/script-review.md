## Changes since September (from the newsletters)
- **Investigators:** DCSA's background-investigation "Special Agents" are now called **Investigators**, and they carry a DCSA credential card only, with no badge (October 2026). That's in Part 2; the verification number is unchanged.
- **Where FSOs start requests:** FSOs now start investigation requests in **DISS** (September 2026). The narration never names the FSO's system, so nothing changes.
- **FBI Rap Back:** real-time alerts about new criminal records are now the cornerstone of continuous vetting for industry (August 2026). That's in Part 4.
- **Citizenship documents:** your FSO can check them in person or digitally (July 2026). That's in Part 1.
- **Not mentioned:** none of the July–October issues says the applicant portal's self-reporting feature has launched, or gives new PVQ dates. So the series keeps "report to your FSO" and "the PVQ is being phased in".
